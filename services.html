A typical project

It usually starts with “this keeps happening.”

Leads are sitting without follow-up. Onboarding is waiting for logins. Nobody notices a field is blank until the dashboard is wrong. A task disappears when one person misses a meeting. Customers receive duplicate messages. A subscription changes in one tool but not another.

I trace the operational cause, decide what should happen instead, and then build the simplest system that can reliably support that process.

Good fit

I’m probably useful if...

  • You already use GoHighLevel or another CRM but the process around it is inconsistent.
  • Your team keeps chasing clients, logins, approvals, missing information, or internal follow-up manually.
